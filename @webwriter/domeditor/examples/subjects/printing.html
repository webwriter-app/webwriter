The Diamond Sutra is printed in China

This long scroll was printed from woodblocks. Its closing colophon records a date equivalent to 11 May 868 and names Wang Jie as its sponsor. It is the earliest complete printed book known with a date.

Look closely: How does the long scroll format arrange image and text?

 Jikji is printed in Korea with movable metal type

The Korean book Jikji, printed in Cheongju, is the oldest surviving book made with movable metal type. It predates the Gutenberg Bible by about 78 years. The surviving volume is held in the National Library of France.

Compare: Woodblock printing makes a page from a carved block; movable type lets a printer rearrange separate characters. What kinds of work might each method require?

Library of Congress, “From Jikji to Gutenberg”.

 The Gutenberg Bible is printed in Mainz

Gutenberg and his workshop printed the Bible using movable metal type. It was one of the first major books made this way in Western Europe. Printers produced the text, and artisans often added color and decoration by hand afterward.

Look closely: Find the repeated columns of type. Which letters or decorations appear to have been added by hand?
