Water – a natural remedy
for annoying heartburn?

Heartburn can be really annoying. It is caused by food remains and stomach juices getting up into the throat. These juices are comparable to hydrochloric acid at a concentration of ~0.1moll. There are medicines to treat heartburn, but there is also a whole range of home remedies and tricks that you can find in lots of places. Today you will investigate one of them. 

Take a look at these two statements from the online material of two German health insurance companies:

How is heartburn treated with medicines?

There are three different ways to treat heartburn:

1)  The production of stomach acid is inhibited by so-called 
      proton pump inhibitors.
2)  H2 receptor blockers have a similar effect,
      reducing the production of acid.
3)  Antacids chemically bind the stomach acid 
      that is already in the stomach.
 
Number 3 is the most interesting one for us. Antacids usually come as tablets (see example). The active ingredient is sodium hydrogen carbonate (baking soda). The molecule is shown on the right (in solution). 

Try out how the two remedies work:

Both large beakers contain a 0.1 M HCl solution, which models stomach acid. Next to them are a flask of water and a test tube holding the amount of sodium hydrogen carbonate (NaHCO3) found in a standard shop-bought tablet (< 1 g).  Add each of them to one of the beakers and observe the effect. 

Let’s take another look at the statements:

One of them actually talks about diluting and the other about neutralising. So which is it? You can answer this question with your observations.

Are you ready?


It dilutes, and the pH changes only a little...  So water has no effect??? Is it all a scam??? 

Not necessarily. In the experiment we assumed 100 ml of HCl solution each time. The real amount, however, is much smaller – depending on the person, less than 1 ml in the throat can be enough to cause heartburn symptoms.
