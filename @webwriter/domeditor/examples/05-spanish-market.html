A picnic at the mercado

Your mission

Your group is shopping for a picnic in a fictional Spanish-speaking town. Practise greeting a seller, asking for food, and checking a total. You will use quiero (I want), cuesta (it costs), and por favor (please).

1. Listen to each other

Work in pairs. Read each scene aloud, then swap roles. Use the slide arrows to move through the conversation.

Saludar · Greet

Cliente: ¡Hola! Buenos días.
Vendedor: Buenos días. ¿Qué quieres?

Notice how both speakers greet each other before ordering.

Pedir · Order

Cliente: Quiero dos manzanas y un pan, por favor.
Vendedor: Aquí tienes.

Point to the items as you read. Change dos to tres on a second reading.

Pagar · Pay

Cliente: ¿Cuánto cuesta?
Vendedor: Cuesta cuatro euros.
Cliente: Gracias. ¡Adiós!

Check the prices below: two apples cost €2 and one bread costs €2.

2. Choose your picnic

Fictional market prices per item

You have €8 for two people. Buy at least one of each type of food. Agree on a shopping list, calculate the total, and act out your new dialogue. The seller should check the buyer’s arithmetic before taking pretend payment.

3. Check the language

Complete the polite request.

Quiero un pan, por favor.

Which reply answers “¿Cuánto cuesta?”?

 Buenos días. Cuesta cuatro euros. ¡Adiós!

Exit ticket: Say one phrase you could use with a new food word. Your partner answers without reading the slides.
