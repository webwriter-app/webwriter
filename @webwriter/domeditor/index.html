Invite your learners

Turn on sharing in the preview. Learners join with a link or QR code, without an account, and their work goes straight to your browser.

Work alone or in groups

For each part of the document, decide whether learners get their own copy, share one in pairs or larger groups, or all work on the same one.

Choose what learners see and change

Hide parts meant only for you. Then choose which changes learners may make, from editing text to moving, styling, or adding widgets.

Share a file

Download your document as one HTML file that opens in any browser, widgets included. Choose Offline HTML to bundle everything for use without an internet connection.

Print it

Print your document or save it as a PDF. Slides, boards, and canvases are arranged for paper, and widgets can provide their own print view.
