Click selects, Shift or Ctrl click adds, Shift and a drag on the background draws a marquee; a plain drag there pans, and Ctrl and the wheel zooms. Drag a gate to move the selection, drag from an output port to an input port to connect, drag the handles of the selected note to resize it, drag the legend. By keyboard: Tab to an item, Space selects, the arrow keys move the selection (Shift for larger steps; Enter commits, Escape takes it back). Tab to an output port, Enter picks a wire up, the arrow keys choose the input, Enter connects. By touch: tap selects, a long press adds, a long press on the background and a drag draws the marquee.
